FAQ

Straightanswers.

The questions I get asked before every engagement, answered the way I would answer them on a call.

What is AEO, and is it different from SEO?

AEO (answer engine optimisation, sometimes GEO) is about being the source an AI model cites when it answers a question, rather than a link a person clicks. The technical foundations overlap heavily with SEO. What differs is the emphasis: clear entities, structured data, quotable claims, and third-party corroboration. I run them together because they feed each other.

How long before I see results?

Technical and on-page fixes can move things inside 30 to 60 days. Content and authority compound over three to six months. Anyone promising page one in a fortnight is selling you something else.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes, and they are often the best fit. A small site with no legacy debt can be structured correctly from the start, which is far cheaper than untangling it at Series B.

How do you measure AI visibility?

A tracked set of buyer questions, run repeatedly across the major engines, scored on whether you are mentioned, cited, or recommended, and against whom. That baseline becomes the scoreboard we work from.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope and how much of the execution sits with me versus your team. The audit call is free, and you get the findings whether or not we work together.

Do you take on retainers or projects?

Both. Fixed-scope projects for audits, migrations and programmatic builds. Monthly retainers where content and authority need to compound. Most people start with an audit and decide from there.
