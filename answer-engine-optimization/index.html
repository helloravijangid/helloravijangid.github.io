The four steps.

Baseline

Where you actually stand, on Google and inside AI answers. A fixed prompt set run across every major engine, scored on mention, citation and recommendation, against competitors you name.

Map the gap

The roadmap gets sequenced by citation gap and revenue, not by search volume. You get a short prioritised list, not a hundred row spreadsheet nobody opens twice.

Build

Technical fixes, entity and schema work, AI extractable content and internal structure, shipped. I work in your stack alongside your team, and ship most changes without waiting on a developer.

Automate and measure

Agents wired to live Search Console, GA4 and rank data take over the monitoring. Rankings, citations per engine, and where both land in your pipeline, reported in one page in plain language.
