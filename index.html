01 Why now

The result page stopped being the destination.

A growing share of research now finishes inside an AI answer. No click, no session, no line in your analytics — but the buying decision already happened.

If a model does not have a clear, well-sourced picture of what you do and who you are for, it recommends whoever it does understand. Usually that is the competitor who published the comparison page you never wrote. Classic SEO still decides what gets crawled and trusted. AI search decides what gets repeated. They are one problem and they need one strategy.
