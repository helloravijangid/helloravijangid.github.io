Three waysI work.

Most engagements combine all three. Where we start depends on what the baseline finds, not on what is easiest to sell you.

Pick the gap costing you most.

Each one stands alone. Together they compound, which is why most engagements end up using all three.

S/01

AI search visibility

Getting cited, recommended and quoted by the engines your buyers now ask first. Measured against named competitors, not assumed.

  • Visibility baseline across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Overviews
  • Citation gap mapping through repeated prompt testing
  • Entity and knowledge graph building
  • Schema, markdown twins and llms.txt
  • Placements in the sources models already trust
S/02

SEO for SaaS and startups

The foundation everything else rests on. A site that crawls cleanly, targets terms buyers convert on, and turns reading into signups.

  • Technical audit and crawl health
  • Buyer intent keyword and topical cluster mapping
  • Pillar content, briefs and refresh cycles
  • Core Web Vitals and conversion work
S/03

Programmatic and automation

Scale without a bigger team. Page systems built from your own data, and agents that keep the analysis running continuously.

  • Opportunity and template modelling
  • Programmatic page generation from structured data
  • Indexation and thin content guards
  • Agents wired to live search data
  • Decay pruning, anomaly detection and reporting
